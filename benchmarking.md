# Benchmarking Guidelines

Never benchmark an empty, near-empty, or artificially trivial room for performance evaluation. Performance benchmarks must contain at least 30 active entities per room, with a maximum of 80 entities. Players and bots share a pool of 10 maximum entities. The workload must always include projectiles and exercise representative physics, AI, networking, and combat activity. Every benchmark must report component-level telemetry sufficient to distinguish orchestration/process overhead from simulation, AI, physics, networking, projectile, and other significant costs. A benchmark that does not exercise a practically representative room must be explicitly classified as an infrastructure/smoke test and must not be used for capacity or architectural conclusions.
